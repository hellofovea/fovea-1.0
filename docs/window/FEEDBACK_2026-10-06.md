# App UI feedback, Oct 6: the dev team's points, the decisions, and why

The dev team reviewed the window and Settings design in `App UI 反馈.xlsx` (37 rows). This file
records, for each row:
- what the devs found and proposed;
- what Yulun decided;
- what is now built in the Fovea Captures prototype, and what is still open.

Rows are numbered as in the spreadsheet. Row 1 is the header. Rows 2, 14, 20, 23, 26, 33 and 35
are group titles.

**The rule for this round:** keep it simple. Every addition is built on the existing design; nothing
was redesigned. It stays macOS-native: system sheets, menus, lists, segmented controls and alerts.
Where a choice was unclear, we looked at how shipping apps handle it (references are listed under
each row).

**To preview:** run `cd Prototypes && swift run FoveaCaptures`.
- **States menu:** shows each of row 13's fifteen states, plus Memory and Dictionary states, where
  each appears.
- **Help › Report a Problem…** opens row 9's sheet.
- **Shots:** `swift run FoveaCaptures --snapshot shots --only fb-` renders every addition, written
  to `Prototypes/shots/captures/`:
  - `fb-set-…`: Settings;
  - `fb-sheet-…`: the sheets;
  - `fb-window-…`: the window's errors;
  - `fb-detail-…`, `fb-tag`, `fb-site`, `fb-viewer`: the window.

## For the team: a requirement and two rows still to decide

**Requirement, row 19: Fovea learns the user's vocabulary.** It must learn words from its own
transcription errors and from the edits the user makes afterwards. It is no longer "later, maybe".
What it has to do:
1. **Notice a correction.** After Fovea pastes its words, watch the field it typed into for a short
   while. When the user replaces a word or phrase Fovea wrote, keep the pair: what Fovea wrote, and
   what the user changed it to.
2. **Decide whether it is vocabulary.** Learn a name, term, acronym or spelling: Lin, not "Lynn";
   api-server; 主仓. Ignore a rewrite, a change of mind, or a style edit.
3. **Add the word to the dictionary as Learned (✦).** Keep what was heard ("heard as Lynn"), where
   it happened (the app), and when. The prototype's dictionary already shows learned words this
   way.
4. **Use it the next time,** in every mode (row 15).
5. **Keep the user in charge.** A learned word can be removed like any other. The dictionary's
   footer explains where learned words come from. Nothing about the person is inferred beyond the
   word itself.

The devs proposed hiding the Learned category until the feature exists. Instead, Learned and ✦ stay
in the design, because the feature is required. Until it ships, only the empty-state copy must not
promise it. The prototype's empty state doesn't.

**Still to decide** (the only rows Yulun hasn't answered; a row the devs marked 待讨论 but Yulun
answered is decided, details filled in by the prototype included):
- **Row 12: how usage is counted, and where it is explained.** Yulun can't settle this alone. It
  goes with pricing. The prototype keeps the placeholder: credits per month; dictation free;
  capture and dispatch use credits.
- **Row 7: the classic capsule.** What it is is explained under row 7. Keep it or not is still open.

For the team meeting, these two rows are in Chinese, with the devs' words and screenshots, in the
open-questions page.

**Decided on Oct 7:** row 6 (the language you speak stays a setting) and row 29 (answered by the
three-day home page, Memory's search and the dictionary's list).

**Ignored:**
- 11 (Help menu contents)

**Not relevant to this design:** rows 36 and 37. They stay as designed.

## 15 · How Fovea writes up what you said

**Devs:** the Mac app's three modes (Faithful, Polished, Smart, default Smart). Keep them? Which
default?

**Decided again (Oct 7), replacing what follows:** no setting. Fovea writes up what you said one
way, **Auto**, the Mac app's "智能": Faithful in code editors and terminals, Polished everywhere
else. Voice & Capture › Writing is removed, and so are the modes in a capture's "Transcribe Again",
which is now one action that uses the same rule. The record below is the Oct 6 decision, kept for
history.

**Decided:**
- Keep a choice, but **Polished is the default.**
- **Polished** means:
  - fillers are removed;
  - stumbles are tolerated, so not every word is transcribed;
  - a change of mind midway is handled (the smart part).
- The other three stay available and must be easy to move between.

**Built** in Voice & Capture › **Writing**, one segmented control, Polished first:

| Mode | What it does | "Um, so let's move the review to Thursday at three, no, wait, four, and, uh, ask Lin to bring the notch study." |
|---|---|---|
| **Polished** (default, new) | Drops the ums, the false starts and the words you take back. When you change your mind halfway, only what you meant is written. | Let's move the review to Thursday at four, and ask Lin to bring the notch study. |
| **Faithful** (the Mac app's) | Proofreads only: punctuation, misheard words and fillers are fixed. Nothing is reworded or moved. | So let's move the review to Thursday at three, no, wait, four, and ask Lin to bring the notch study. |
| **Rewritten** (the Mac app's "润色") | Turns speech into writing: smooth sentences, laid out. Your meaning, your negations and your terms stay exact. | Let's move the review to Thursday at 4:00. Lin, please bring the notch study. |
| **Auto** (the Mac app's "智能") | Picks for the app you're in: Faithful for code editors and terminals, Polished everywhere else. | In Mail, as Polished writes it; in Xcode or Terminal, as Faithful does. |

- The same sentence is written each way under the control ("You say / Fovea writes"), so the
  difference reads at a glance.
- **Naming:** the Mac app's "Polished" (润色) is renamed **Rewritten**, so the new default can take
  the name Polished. The backend needs a fourth mode, or the old Smart table needs to target the new
  Polished.
- **For the devs:** the Aug 16 test found Polished weaker after the model change; worth checking
  again when the new mode is built. Auto's app table stays as it is.

## 24 · Memory, redesigned

**Devs:** add search with bulk delete, multi-select delete with undo, delete by tag, hide, recently
deleted, and detail. Keep Memory Link? A page of its own?

**Decided** (Yulun): redesign it.
- **Areas of your life:** split what Fovea knows into areas (work, personal finances,
  interests…). Each gets a summary inferred from what the user sends their agents, as ChatGPT and
  Claude present memory.
- **The raw database:** a raw-memory database, holding each memory and the source it came from,
  collapsed behind one button that leads to memory search.
- **Management:** accept most of the devs' management features.

**Built** (Settings › Memory, under the Dictionary):
1. **About you:** one card per area (Work, How you work, Interests, Personal finances). Each card
   has:
   - its tile and name, and when it was last updated;
   - the summary, in Fovea's words;
   - the **tags it reads from, with their counts**. Each tag opens the window filtered to it.
   - A ⋯ menu: **Edit Summary…** (yours wins from then on) and **Forget "Area"…** (the summary
     goes; the memories it came from stay until deleted).
2. **All memories · N memories** with **Search Memories…**, which opens a sheet. The sheet is a
   native list:
   - search by words or tags;
   - **All / Hidden from Agents / Recently Deleted**;
   - ⇧/⌘-click and ⌘A selection;
   - **Delete** (⌘⌫) with **Undo**;
   - **Hide from Agents / Show to Agents**;
   - **Restore**, with the days left of 30;
   - the selected memory's detail: its words, the words it came from ("From what you said" / "You
     asked Fovea to remember"), when and where, its tags, and **Show Capture**.
   - Deleting by tag is a search for the tag, ⌘A, ⌘⌫.
3. **Remembering:**
   - **Memory Link** (on): remember what you send your agents, not only what you ask. Off pauses
     it, and About you says so.
   - Remember things when you ask.
   - Show when Fovea remembers something.
   - **Access Log…:** which agent read your memory, when and what it asked; read-only, filtered
     All/Search/Recall/List.

Screenshot retention stays in Voice & Capture › History (30/90/180 days/Forever, default 90).

**References:**
- Claude's memory: topics you can read, edit and delete one by one; pause and reset.
- ChatGPT: saved memories, managed in a list.
- Fovea Mac's own Memory page: the access log and the hidden and deleted states.

**Rules:** this is an inferred profile, which AGENTS.md forbids ("No stored 'learned facts' or
inferred user profiles"). It is Yulun's explicit decision, so AGENTS.md needs amending before it
ships.

## Oct 7 · After the first look

Yulun's fixes after opening the prototype:
- **The home page shows the last three days** (today and the two before), not the month. Other days
  are on the board above. The filter menu offers Last 3 days (the default) or Today.
- **Closing the expanded card has no animation.** It used to shrink back into its place in the
  grid, taking the whole window through grey. The X (or Esc, or a click outside) now closes it at
  once. The picture viewer's X does the same.
- **The recording and Open in the agent were small pills at the bottom right** (rows 27 and 28),
  with the tags at the left of the same line. Later the same day both were removed from every
  card; the foot is the tags alone.
- **Screen awareness** (Voice & Capture, a new section; not a row of the sheet). Yulun asked
  whether dictation sees the screen: people need to know whether theirs is being captured, and to
  choose. It is built as row 22 built questions, item by item:
  - **What you pick** (text you select, the regions and files you take): Always.
  - **The window you're speaking into** (its app, title, web address or file): a switch, on.
  - **The text in that window**: a picture of the window, read on this Mac; only its text goes with
    the words. A switch, on, shown while the window is allowed.
  - The note says it plainly: "Never the whole screen, unless you take it." Permissions' Screen
    Recording row adds "and to read the window you're speaking into" while that is on.
  - **For the devs, what the Mac app does today** (`fovea-mac`, read Oct 7). Every fn dictation
    takes a picture of the window in front at key-down, and of each window switched to while
    speaking (`VoiceFlowFrameBuffer`, started unconditionally in `RecordingStateManager` at the
    start of a transcript). Vision reads its text on the Mac, and up to 2,000 characters of it go
    to `/api/voiceflow/scene/prefetch` and `/api/transcribe` with the app, title and URL. With
    Memory Link on, the picture itself is kept and sent to `/api/memory/organize` (terminals and
    IDEs excepted). There is no setting for any of it, and nothing on screen says so. The new
    switches must gate the picture, the text and the uploads; with the window off, only the app's
    bundle ID stays, locally, to type into it.
  - **Two bugs found on the way**, for questions: "Screen context" Minimal doesn't stop the window
    pictures the attention pipeline sends to `/api/quickanswer/attention/stream`, and "Full" does
    nothing more than Balanced. Diagnostics read a bool `context_sharing` instead of the string
    `settings.contextSharing`.
- **Credits are shown as the Mac shows a capacity.** The thin full-width bar is gone. Apple's
  guidelines (Gauges › macOS) use a level indicator for a value in a range: a translucent track
  filling with a solid bar, green by default, its color changing near the top of the range. Plan's
  This month is now a System Settings row: credits left and what's used of the total at the left,
  the Mac's own capacity indicator, 180 pt wide, at the right. It turns yellow past 70 % and red past
  90 %. The avatar's hover uses the same control. Progress bars are for work under way, so neither
  uses one.
