each point:
- what the devs proposed;
- what Yulun decided, over three rounds the same day;
- where the two differ, and why;
- what is now built in the Notch Workbench.

Numbering follows the spreadsheet: row 2 is #1, row 25 is #24.

**A rule from round two:** the spreadsheet is a list of required functions, not a visual reference.
Everything here is built on what Voiceflow and Dispatch Version 2 already are; new pieces are added
to that foundation, never copied from the spreadsheet's images.

**To preview:** run `cd Prototypes && swift run FoveaWorkbench`. The **Feedback · Oct 6** group at
the top of the panel has a tab for each point that has something to see. A tab stages the mock
display, and the flow runs live from there. `--stage=<tab>[:<stage>]` opens straight on a tab; the
tab names are in `FeedbackItem`.

research below, waiting on a decision.

The spec changes these decisions make are in `AMENDMENTS.md` under **Oct 6 feedback**, and they win
over the PRD.

## 1 · Model and thinking effort (with #20)

**Devs:** Fovea today picks a model and reasoning level in two places: Settings ("Answer model") and
a picker on the answer panel. The design had neither.

**Decided:**
- The referents in Choose chat collapse into **one stack**, which fans out under the pointer.
- **Beside the chat pill**, a pill shows the model and effort ("Auto", or "Sonnet 5 · High"). It
  opens a sheet under the row:
  - **Model:** Auto, then the chat's agent's own models.
  - **Thinking:** Auto, then the model's efforts.

**Response:** this agrees with the devs, and amends PRD §12 and §13: the model is no longer hidden.
**Auto** stays the default, so routing is still automatic.

**Open:** does a choice stick for the next request? The Workbench keeps it until it is changed.

## 2 · Errors on the island

**Decided:** design every error, on the island, in English.

The details below are a proposal for Yulun to confirm; they follow fovea-mac's own severities.
- **Three moments:**
  - **As the bar opens:** the microphone.
  - **Inside the listening bar, which keeps listening:** Screen Recording off; an image over 20 MB.
  - **After the words went:** everything else.
- **Anatomy:** Paste not found's.
  - a glyph in its tone's color (white, Needs you amber, or Failed red);
  - a title and one line;
  - at most one action: Retry, Open Settings, Sign in, Upgrade, Renew;
  - ✕ in the ring of the time left.
- **Time:** 2.5 s for information, 4 s for a warning, 8 s with an action; paused under the pointer.
- **Retryable errors** say "Your recording is saved".

## 3, 8 · Text, files and screenshots while speaking

**Devs:** selected text had no design. The screenshot key conflicts (double-tap Option is Claude
desktop's Quick Entry), and the design differed from the shipping app.

**Round one:** Option took screenshots and ⌘ picked text. The research flagged ⌘'s conflicts: the
⌘-click habit, Japanese ⌘英かな, Raycast's double right ⌘.

**Decided (round two):**
- **Option is the highlighter.**
  - A press (or a hold) turns the pointer into a highlighter in **the accent color the person chose
    in Fovea** (Workbench panel › Fovea accent).
  - A drag across text marks the words it crosses, line by line, as a marker would.
  - A click marks a whole paragraph, or a desktop file.
  - The marks stay on the screen while the session lasts. What was marked joins the stack.
  - One press, one highlight: the highlighter puts itself down after.
- **Round three:** the pointer only changes color, an arrow in the accent color with no highlighter
  icon drawn above it. The old icon flickered because two cursor rules fought; there is one now
  (`screenCursor`).
- **⌘ takes screenshots.** One press takes one region; two presses take the whole screen. Held,
  capture is on while held.
- Text, files and screenshots ride **the same stack**. Opened, they lie side by side.

**The keys today:**

| Key | On a voice bar | Elsewhere |
|---|---|---|
| fn | Voiceflow: start, then stop (transcribe) | starts Voiceflow |
| fn ⌃ | Dispatch: start, then stop; on Choose chat, send | starts Dispatch |
| ⌘ (either side), press | One screenshot: drag a region | the app's |
| ⌘, two presses | The whole screen | the app's |
| Option (left), press | The highlighter: drag across words, or click a paragraph or a file | the app's |
| Left ⌃ alone | Choose chat: every chat, and back | the app's |
| Esc | Steps out one level (capture, highlighter, sheet, then the request) | — |

**Still worth knowing:** a lone ⌘ press is common on the way to a shortcut. The Workbench counts a
press only when nothing else is pressed or clicked during it, which avoids ⌘-Tab and ⌘-click. It
cannot tell a ⌘ meant for Fovea from one meant for ⌘英かな. Letting people pick the key in Settings
covers that.

**For the devs:** the product needs the selection under the highlighter. Accessibility gives text
ranges and their bounds (`AXBoundsForRange`) in most apps; Vision can read text in pictures and
PDFs where it doesn't.

## 9–10, 17 · The control board

**Devs:**
- There's no agent screen to show; remove the screens.
- Fovea can't know every Claude Code session's status, so list only Fovea's own.
- Fovea can't say which files or code an agent is editing.

**Decided (round two):**
- The board should cover every running session on the Mac (#17).
- The round-one designs were disturbing: icons on every row, a focus pane, a progress card. Redo
  it, as macOS-native and as minimal as possible, using only what can really be had.

**Research:** the best lists are text first.
- Apple's menu-bar menus, Claude Code's Agent View, and the Codex app (no leading icons).
- "Running" without details is motion in a small slot: the HIG's unlabeled spinner, an age that
  ticks.
- An unknown status gets a quiet look of its own and never moves (Agent View dims idle sessions;
  Live Activities have `isStale`; Linear has a `stale` state).

**Round two built** a text-only menu: 28 pt rows of title, project and one word, no icons. Round
three found it too bare to read at a glance.

**Decided (round three):** the board is still the biggest problem. It should be easy to look at
(icons, illustration and deliberate design that make the words easier to read) while staying
informative. Research macOS-native and ambient designs and notch products such as coucou.

**Research (round three):** 48 references, among them coucou (run on this Mac), Vibe Island, Open
Island, Claude Island, Notchi, Claude Statistics, Claude Code's Agent View, CodexMonitor, Linear,
Apple's Live Activities and notifications, NotchNook, Atoll and Raycast.
- **Who before what.** A 20–26 pt brand icon is the fastest identity cue (Notification Center,
  NotchNook). The round-one problem was 40 pt pictures, not icons.
- **Two lines, two weights.** A semibold title over a quieter regular line scans; title and project
  on one line compete.
- **State is read once:** a section or a fixed glyph, never scattered (Agent View, CodexMonitor).
- **Only running moves; color only for action:** amber for needs-you, red for failed, everything
  else in the island's ink.
- **Approve in place,** inside the row (Claude Island, Vibe Island, coucou).
- **Illustration only where it carries state.** Mascots and pixel scenes (Notchi, coucou) are
  charming but cost a third of the panel and add nothing to read; coucou also renders code.
- **No counts, chips or model names.** Vibe Island's chips and Open Island's "Show all 3" are what
  makes them noisy, and the island's rules forbid them.

**Built: two designs, one switch in the tab (Sections, Badges).** Both are 400 pt wide, with the
same rows and the same order; they differ only in where the state lives.
- **Order:** the hover list's (PRD §8).
  1. **Needs you:** a question, then a failure.
  2. **Done:** not yet opened.
  3. **Running.**
  4. **Status unavailable.**
- **Rows** are 46 pt and two lines, led by the agent's icon.
  - **Title:** 13.5 semibold.
  - **Second line:** what the agent reliably reports.
    - Needs you: the question, in amber, its command set in code.
    - Failed: the word in red, then why.
    - Done: the first sentence of the agent's answer.
    - Running: the project.
    - Status unavailable: the project and when it was last active.
  - **At the right:** Deny and Allow in place; for a running session, the turning ring and how long
    it has run (Stop under the pointer); otherwise how long ago it finished (a chevron under the
    pointer).
- **Sections:** a header over each part, with an 11 pt glyph over the icons' column (the hand in
  amber, the ring, the check, the question mark) and its word in line with the titles. The hand nods
  when a session joins Needs you.
- **Badges:** no headers; the parts are split by a gap and a hairline. A 12 pt badge on each icon's
  corner carries the state, cut out of the icon by the island's black: amber "!", a red cross, a
  white check, or the dark turning ring.
- **Status unavailable:** the icon gray and faint, the title dimmed, no badge, nothing moving.
- **Size:** six rows show, ending on a whole row; past that the list scrolls. No counts.
- **Moves:** a session that changes state moves to its section on a spring. A click opens it; ↑↓ and
  Return work as in a menu.
- **Recommended: Sections.** It matches Claude Code's Agent View, state is read once per part, and
  usually only two parts show. Badges is calmer per row and more Apple-like, and is the fallback if
  the headers feel heavy.

**Round four: four more designs, after a wider survey.**

**Decided:** not yet satisfied with the board. Look properly at the notch apps around now, which
take very different forms, and show the variations.

**Survey:** 116 screenshots of 30+ apps, several screens of each.
- **Agent notch apps:** Pushary, Crew Tower, an Atoll fork with agents, NotchHUD, coder-island,
  CodeIsland, Ping Island, Chimlo, Notchcode, Notch Pilot, Droppy, AgentPeek, AgentNotch, MioIsland,
  ClaudeNotch, and more states of Vibe Island, Notchi and coucou.
- **General notch apps:** Boring Notch, Alcove, DynamicLake, NotchFlow, Atoll, MewNotch, NotchDrop,
  DynamicNotchKit, NotchNook.
- **Apple:** the HIG's Live Activities; iPhone Dynamic Island apps (F1 standings, MLB's two
  activities, Apollo, Flighty, Uber); Siri on macOS.

**What the survey found:**
- Almost every agent notch app uses the same layout: a flat list of two-line rows with chips (agent,
  terminal, model, tokens, cost). The chips are what makes them noisy.
- The ideas that are actually different are rarer:
  - a card that takes over for what waits on you (Pushary, the Atoll fork, Crew Tower);
  - separate bubbles (Droppy, the Dynamic Island's detached minimal);
  - wings beside the notch (DynamicLake, Pushary);
  - two panes (Boring Notch, Droppy).
- Done with the answer inline is rare: only Chimlo, Open Island and coucou show it.
- Unreachable sessions: NotchHUD's "Host unavailable", the Atoll fork's "idle (stale)".

**Built:** the 9–10, 17 tab's Design menu now has six designs, all showing the same sessions.
Sections and Badges are as in round three. The four new ones are drawn at the Spacious scale, all
in the island's black.

1. **Focus.** The most pressing session gets a card:
   - its agent, title, and agent and project;
   - an amber "Needs you" tag;
   - the question at 17 pt;
   - Deny and Allow as two 36 pt halves of the row.

   With nothing waiting, the card shows what came back (Read the answer, Open in its app), or how
   long it has run in large figures with what was asked. Every other session sits on a shelf
   underneath, one line each.
2. **Capsules.** The island stays the size of the notch. Each session is its own black capsule
   under it, dropping out one after another:
   - the one waiting on you is tallest, with an amber edge and its buttons;
   - a done one shows its answer's first sentence;
   - sessions that can't be read are narrower and dimmed.

   The pointer anywhere on the capsules keeps the board open.
3. **Wings.** The sessions sit as icons in the notch's wings, the most pressing nearest the notch:
   - your turn on the left (waiting, then done), the rest on the right (running, then unreadable);
   - the state is a ring round each icon: amber, a turning quarter, a check, red, or dashed;
   - a full wing ends in "⋯", never a number.

   The session under the pointer is read on a stage below, the same card as Focus.
4. **Split.** The sessions are listed on the left, one line each. The one under the pointer is read on
   the right: its question and buttons, its answer, or its time and what was asked. It uses
   Dispatch's 660 pt width.

`--stage=progress:<design>` opens on one. `--render-board=<dir>` draws all six off screen, in both
densities, for when the screen is locked. `Prototypes/shots/board-designs.png` shows the six side by
side.

**A session opened while it works** shows its request, then one quiet line: the ring, Working, and
the time it has run (m:ss). Under it are the bar and the box to ask more. A session whose state
can't be read says "Status unavailable · last active 2h ago".

**How every session is found (#17):**
- **Claude Code:** `claude agents --json` (v2.1.145+) lists every live session with `busy` /
  `waiting` / `idle` and what it waits for, with nothing to install. Hooks are an opt-in for instant
  events and approvals.
  - **Caveat:** the `claude` on this Mac's PATH is v2.1.92, too old.
- **Codex:** `thread/list` for the threads Fovea's own app-server holds. Others need Codex hooks
  (the person approves them in `/hooks`) or reading its session files.
- **Anything else** goes in the third group, never with a guessed state.

**Line counts, for discussion.** The devs say Fovea can't get them. The sources say it can:
- **Claude Code:** an Edit or Write result carries `structuredPatch` and, when available,
  `gitDiff {additions, deletions}` (`tool_use_result` in stream-json; the `PostToolUse` hook's
  `tool_response`).
- **Codex:** `fileChange` items carry each file's diff, and `turn/diff/updated` sends one unified
  diff for the whole turn.
- **Either agent:** `git diff --numstat` in the session's folder gives exact counts, and also
  catches edits made through shell commands.

Sources: code.claude.com/docs/en/headless, /hooks, /agent-sdk; openai/codex `app-server-protocol`.
Until that is agreed, the Workbench shows file names only.

## 14 · The folder a new chat starts in (research, for a decision)

**Devs:** "new chats can only be created in a new folder, not an existing one."

**Verdict:** not true of the agents. It's true of Fovea's code today, and of two narrow hand-off
paths. Evidence is from fovea-mac (`feat/fov208…`) and the agents' sources.

**Fovea's code:**
- Every new chat gets a private folder:
  - Codex: `…/Fovea/codex-quickanswer/<id>` (`CodexAppServerBridge.swift:3717-3728`, `:4699`);
  - Claude Code: `…/claude-quickanswer/<id>` (`ClaudeCodeSessionBridge.swift:842`, `:873`);
  - Cursor: `…/cursor/conversations/<id>` (`CursorACPBridge.swift:632-646`).
- The bridge already runs a thread in any folder it is given (`:2533-2539`, `thread/start {cwd}`).
  But a folder only arrives together with an existing session id
  (`CodexQuickAnswerProvider.swift:1101`).
- The "new conversation" destination carries no folder (`QuickAnswerDestination.swift:457`).
- The PRDs chose this on purpose:
  - `QuickAnswer-Codex-App-Server-PRD-2026-09-01.md:196` allows a folder the person chose, and
    forbids guessing one from a window title;
  - `QuickAnswerConversationDirectory.swift:377`: "Fovea has no project chooser for Quick Answer".
- Side effect: each Fovea chat has become its own Codex "project" in `~/.codex/config.toml`.

**What the agents allow:**
- **Codex:** `thread/start`, `resume` and `fork` all take any folder. There's no trust prompt: the
  app-server marks the repo trusted itself. The thread then shows in Codex Desktop and `codex resume`
  for that project.
- **Claude Code:** the folder is the process's own. `-p` shows no trust prompt. Sessions are kept
  per folder, but `-p` sessions stay out of the session picker whatever their folder; open one with
  `claude --desktop --resume <id>`.
- **Cursor:** ACP's `session/new {cwd}` takes any absolute folder.

**The real limits:**
- Fovea's link to Codex Desktop can only continue a thread Desktop holds, not create one.
- Claude Desktop's `claude://resume` link only reopens a session.
- `-p` sessions don't appear in Claude's picker anywhere.

**Recommendation:**
- Give "new chat" a folder: `newConversation(provider, workspace:)`.
- Take the folder from the person's choice or confirmation, never a guess. Candidates: folders
  recently used in Codex and Claude, the front window's document.
- Codex is a small change: pass `thread/start {cwd: project}`.
- Claude Code: start the process in the project.
- Cursor: pass `session/new {cwd}`, and stop writing Fovea's rules file into the person's repo.
- **Costs to weigh:**
  - Codex will mark the repo trusted.
  - The project's own settings and `CLAUDE.md` will load (that's the point).
  - The agent can now edit the person's repo.

**For Yulun:** confirm, and the Workbench's Choose chat gets "New chat in <project>".

### Oct 7: the project label, and new chats in the person's own folder

**Devs (Oct 7):** the project tag beside a session's title can only partly be shown. A new question
runs in Fovea's temporary folder. Only a continued Codex session has a real project folder.

**Verdict:** right about Fovea's code today, not about the agents. More detail:
- **Fovea's code:** Fovea even resumes Claude sessions into its own folder.
- **The agents** take any folder:
  - Codex: `thread/start {cwd}`; the bridge already passes `workingDirectory` when it has one.
  - Claude Code: the process's folder.
  - Cursor: `session/new {cwd}`.
- **Codex's own desktop app** does what Fovea does for chats with no project
  (`~/Documents/Codex/<date>/<slug>`). It uses the project's folder once a project is chosen.

**What other products do:**
- **Their folder:** a project picker, or the folder they were launched from, with a list of recent
  projects read from the agents' own stores. Products: Claude Desktop's Code tab, the Codex app,
  CodexMonitor, Vibe Kanban, Crystal, opcode, Zed, and JetBrains (the open project).
- **The notch apps that start sessions:**
  - Ping Island: a folder picker, behind a flag.
  - Notchy: the front Xcode project.
  - A NotchPilot fork: the last folder used.
  - Pushary and MioIsland: from the phone, with recent projects.
- **The label:** every one names the project by its folder's name.
- **Orchestrator apps start the agent in the person's folder the same ways:** Codex's
  `thread/start {cwd}`, or the agent's process started in that folder. Examples: CodexMonitor,
  Vibe Kanban, Happy, Crystal and Nimbalyst, Claude Squad. Most put a task that edits code in its own
  worktree.

**What it costs:**
- **Codex trusts the folder silently.** `thread/start` with write access marks an untrusted repo
  trusted (app-server "Project trust", #16492).
- **Claude runs project hooks and connects `.mcp.json` servers.** In an untrusted folder, `-p` and
  the SDK still run them (code.claude.com/docs/en/permissions).
- **Project rules load.** CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md and `.cursor/rules` take effect, which is the point,
  though they can clash with Fovea's instructions.
- **Claude's own history hides the chat.** `-p` and SDK sessions stay out of Claude's session picker
  (docs, changelog 2.1.90). They can still be opened by id: `claude --desktop --resume <id>`.

**Options, ranked:**
1. **The referents' project.** Take the folder from the window the capture came from and walk up to
   its git root:
   - VS Code and Cursor: `AXDocument`;
   - Xcode: its document;
   - Terminal and iTerm: the shell's folder;
   - Finder: the front folder.

   The pill reads "New chat · app-ui-v1" (CHOOSE_CHAT.md already asks for this). A question that
   isn't about code keeps the private folder.
2. **Known projects, without asking.** Gather them, most recent first, as Choose chat's project rows:
   - Claude: `~/.claude.json` projects, and the `cwd` of its sessions;
   - Codex: `thread/list` `cwd`, and `config.toml` `[projects]`.
3. **Ask once per project per agent.** This goes with options 1 and 2. Run there without asking
   only where the person already trusts the folder:
   - Claude: `hasTrustDialogAccepted`;
   - Codex: `trust_level = "trusted"`.

   Otherwise ask once ("Let Codex work in app-ui-v1?"), or run the first chat read-only.
4. **Into the agent's own history:**
   - Codex: running in the project is enough for its thread list. The experimental `projectId`
     places the chat under the right project in the app.
   - Claude: "Open in Claude Desktop" (`--desktop --resume`). For Dispatch, `claude --bg --name …`
     puts the session in `claude agents` and the picker.
   - Happy (the mobile Claude client) keeps its SDK sessions in Claude's picker by setting
     `CLAUDE_CODE_ENTRYPOINT`; the picker hides only the `sdk-*` entrypoints. This is undocumented
     and could break.
5. **Later, a worktree per task that edits code.**

## 16 · The activity line

### Oct 7: what a session can show of its steps

**Question:** opened from the board, can a session show the agent's individual steps, or only
"working"? Cross-checked two ways: what Anthropic and OpenAI publish, and the source of 24 notch
apps.

**Sessions Fovea starts.** Both agents report each step live as it happens:
- **Claude Code** (stream-json): `tool_use` with its input, for example `Read{file_path}` and
  `Bash{command, description}`. Edits come back with `structuredPatch`/`gitDiff` line counts.
- **Codex** (app-server): items with parsed actions (`commandActions` read/search/listFiles),
  `fileChange` diffs, the plan (`turn/plan/updated`), commentary marked apart from the answer, and
  reasoning summaries.
- **Not available:** Claude's thinking is hidden by default, and its to-do tools are off by default
  on newer models.
- **Fovea already receives all of this, and drops it on purpose.** Its progress contract keeps only
  the kind of step and its status, by design (`QuickAnswerProgress.swift`).

**Sessions the person started** (their terminal, IDE, or the desktop apps):
- **State, with nothing to install:** Claude has `claude agents --json` (busy, waiting, idle; what it
  waits for). Fovea already follows Codex Desktop's threads, over an undocumented socket.
- **Steps need hooks** that the person agrees to install:
  - Claude: in `~/.claude/settings.json`. They fire in the terminal, the IDE extensions and Desktop.
  - Codex: in `~/.codex/hooks.json`, trusted once in `/hooks`.
- **What the hooks give:** the current step and its object, live; a history; per-edit line counts;
  to-dos; the final answer.
- **What they don't give:** streamed text, Claude's thinking, or a command's output while it runs.

**What the notch apps show:**
- **The current step with its object** ("Edit webhook.ts", "$ cargo test"): most of them, from
  `PreToolUse` hooks. Among them: coucou, Open Island, CodeIsland, Notchi, Notchcode,
  claude-statistics, the Atoll fork, AgentNotch, Vibe Island.
- **A history of 5–30 steps:** Notchi, coucou, Atoll, Notch Pilot, the Claude Island family.
- **+N −M for each edit:** coucou, computed from the edit's input.
- **Plan progress:** CodeIsland, claude-statistics, Crew Tower.
- **What none shows:** streamed text, Claude's thinking, or live command output.
- **What they all need:** a hook installed once with the person's consent. coucou backs up the file,
  shows the change, and writes it only after the person confirms.

**For a decision:**
- "Kind of step only" (above) rested on the object being out of reach. It is within reach.
- One line of current activity with its object ("Editing IslandShape.swift +18 −6") fits AGENTS.md
  as it stands.
- A list of steps in the session would need an amendment to "no execution steps or logs".
- What the notch apps mostly do: one line of current activity per row on the list, and the history
  only after a click into the session. That is close to our rule already.

### Oct 7, decided and built: one step at a time

**Decided (Yulun):** while an agent works, show one step at a time: what it is actually doing and
to what, as far as the notch apps show it. Only Codex and Claude Code are supported.

**Built** (tab "16 · One step at a time": Session or Board; `Feedback/V2Steps.swift`):

  - Reading `IslandShape.swift`; Searching for “menuBarHeight”; Listing a folder.
  - Editing / Creating `NotchGeometry.swift`.

## Research note: coucou

- **What it is:** [Louis-CFM/coucou](https://github.com/Louis-CFM/coucou), MIT code. It is cloned to
  `~/Desktop/things/fovea/coucou` and builds. It has not been launched: its setup edits
  `~/.claude/settings.json` and `~/.codex/hooks.json`, so ask first.
- **It solves:** the step and its object from hooks; approving from the notch (it holds the
  `PermissionRequest` hook open); a safe hook installer.
- **It doesn't solve:**
  - every session (it has one pill per agent);
  - titles;
  - starting a session in a project;
  - jumping to the right terminal.
