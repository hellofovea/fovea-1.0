# Fovea 1.0: one app on the real Mac

On Oct 9 the three prototypes were brought together as one app. They were the onboarding, the Notch
Workbench and Fovea Captures, each on its own and two of them on a mock desktop. The app is
`FoveaV1/`, built as `Fovea.app`. This page lists what was decided and what departs from the
rules in AGENTS.md. How to run it is in `FoveaV1/README.md`.

## Decided with Yulun (Oct 9)

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| One app | `FoveaV1/`, bundle id `app.fovea`, version 1.0. The prototypes stay as they are, for design work. |
| The window | Fovea Captures (Home's cards, Settings), every design variant fixed at its default. **Home's switcher stays as Captures draws it**, in the toolbar ("keep the original design"). |
| The notch | The Workbench's Voiceflow and Dispatch Version 2, **Native** design only, the **Badges** board only. |
| The notch's choices | **Glass:** Light · Dark. **Panels:** Attached · Floating. **Size:** Compact · Spacious (Spacious by default). They're chosen in the first run's Make it yours and in Settings › Notch, and the real notch shows each as it's chosen. |
| Dispatch | Simulated, as in the Workbench: the sessions, their steps and answers are scripted. The words are real; the script is picked by them. |
| The first run's two tasks | Sent to the real Claude Code on this Mac, with the practice agent standing in when it can't answer. |
| Signing in | The first run opens on an email, then "What should we call you?". There's no server, so the email is kept on this Mac. |
| Hello | "Hello, (name)." right after the name, on the first run's hello page and the notch's line. Home's first page also says "Hello, (name)". |
| Your screen | "Clear my screen" is offered and never done without asking. It hides the other apps, and exactly those come back at the end, or when Fovea quits. |
| Highlighter | A picture of what's marked, plus its words where the app gives them through Accessibility. A click on a Finder file takes the file. |
| Home | Only real captures, starting with the first run's (tagged "First run"). No samples. |
| The board | Opens by hover only (left ⌃ alone works inside Choose chat and a session), and never empty: nothing until the first send. |
| Open at login | A switch on "You're set", on by default, also in Settings › Account. |
| The icon | Ryan's revised serif f (the ring's hole smaller), on a solid white tile. Before, the system's light fill shaded it grey toward the foot. |

## Defaults chosen in building it

- **Speech recognition.** Apple's on-device recognition turns words into text, so macOS asks for Speech Recognition right after the microphone. Fovea's own transcription service (`/api/transcribe`) isn't there yet.
- **Cleaning.** Every paste is cleaned: no ums, a capital and a full stop.
- **Pulled-out chats** stay inside the island's full-screen layer, rather than being separate windows.
- **A display without a notch** shows nothing at rest, and the island at the top center while it's open.
- **The first run's stage** is one clear window over the real desktop. It holds the boards, Fovea's own practice windows and the trip's files, and lets clicks through everywhere else. Its practice files live in a temporary folder, so macOS doesn't ask for Desktop access.
