# Fovea 1.0: one app on the real Mac

On Oct 9 the three prototypes were brought together as one app. They were the onboarding, the Notch
Workbench and Fovea Captures, each on its own and two of them on a mock desktop. The app is
`FoveaV1/`, built as `Fovea.app`. This page lists what was decided and what departs from the
rules in AGENTS.md. How to run it is in `FoveaV1/README.md`.

## Defaults chosen in building it

- **Speech recognition.** Apple's on-device recognition turns words into text, so macOS asks for Speech Recognition right after the microphone. Fovea's own transcription service (`/api/transcribe`) isn't there yet.
- **Cleaning.** Every paste is cleaned: no ums, a capital and a full stop.
- **Pulled-out chats** have a layer of their own, the size of the display they're on, which follows the pointer to the display in use (Oct 11); they aren't separate windows (no ⌘` or Mission Control).
- **A display without a notch** shows nothing at rest, and the island at the top center while it's open.
- **The first run's stage** is one clear window over the real desktop. It holds the boards, Fovea's own practice windows and the trip's files, and lets clicks through everywhere else. Its practice files live in a temporary folder, so macOS doesn't ask for Desktop access.
